2Reading: the half-marathon

Read the text and answer the questions.

When the alarm went off at 5:30, Marta dragged herself out of bed and stumbled down the stairs. She had promised herself she would finish the city half-marathon, even though she had never run more than ten kilometres before. At the starting line, hundreds of runners were already pushing towards the front. Marta slipped through the crowd and joined the middle of the pack. The route took them along the river, over two old bridges and through a tunnel where the cheering echoed off the walls. By kilometre fifteen, her legs felt heavy and she nearly gave up. Then a stranger running alongside her said, “Just get past the next lamp post.” She did, and then the next one. Eventually she sprinted across the finish line, collapsed onto the grass and burst into tears of joy. She had never felt so proud.

Summarise Marta’s route in 3–4 sentences. Use at least five movement prepositions.
